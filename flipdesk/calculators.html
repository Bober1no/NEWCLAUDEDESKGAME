Markup, margin & ROI

Markup = profit ÷ cost. Margin = profit ÷ sale price. They are not the same number. A 50% markup is only a 33% margin. Use margin when you compare deals.

Annualised ROI assumes you could repeat the same flip over and over. It shows why a 15% flip done in 5 days beats a 30% flip that took 2 months.
