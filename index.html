Field Manual

Camera

  • Right-drag orbit · Middle-drag pan
  • Wheel zoom · WASD / arrows / screen edge pan
  • Q / E rotate · F focus selection · Home base

Command

  • Left-click / drag select · Shift add to selection
  • Right-click move, or attack an enemy
  • A attack-move · S stop · H hold · P patrol
  • Ctrl+1..9 make group · 1..9 recall

Economy

  • Paper funds infantry · Ink funds ranged
  • Batteries gate tech · Graphite buys upgrades
  • Build Extractors on desk nodes to earn income
  • Build Desk Trays for population space

The desk itself

  • Stand on book stacks for range, damage and sight
  • Build a tower on a water bottle: 40% cheaper, tougher
  • Spilled pens and rulers block movement — tape over them
  • Extractors extend your build radius; creep outward

Hot seat

  • F2 or the ⇄ button hands the desk over
  • Fog of war, HUD and control groups follow the commander
  • Timed turns pause on a handover card between turns
  • Standing orders keep running while it isn't your turn

Everything you see is built from cones, boxes and cylinders. Read the silhouettes.
